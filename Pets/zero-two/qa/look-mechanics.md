# Natural gaze mechanics for 02

Keep both white boots, legs, red uniform torso and the body anchor planted and forward facing in all sixteen directions. Use the neck to yaw and pitch the separate head modestly. Eyes lead and nose/chin follow. Long pink hair, horns and white headband follow the head a little without changing hairstyle or disconnecting. No whole-body turntable, tilted whole sprite or raster warp.

Retain canonical anime eye construction: blue turquoise irises, dark pupils, white sclera, eyelids, highlights and dark upper lashes. Pupils move within the original eyes and the entire head turns enough to make the gaze readable; do not replace eyes.

Up: head tips back, chin/nose aim upward, irises lie in upper sclera. Down: chin dips, upper eyelids narrow and irises aim down. Right and left: modest three-quarter head yaw, nose tip and pupil positions shift toward that screen edge, far eye naturally foreshortens, both eyes remain visible. Torso stays frontal and character height stays stable.

Intermediate poses combine horizontal yaw and vertical pitch smoothly. At upper diagonals the irises and nose point up and sideways; at lower diagonals the irises and chin point down and sideways. Keep face readable, no back view, no hair covering face, no lost eyes. Use viewer coordinates and fixed clockwise order.
